# SQL Practical Task Sheet (Data Analyst Edition - Till Joins)

## Tasks

1. Create a new database suitable for storing business sales data.  

2. Create four related tables — customers, products, orders, and order_details — with proper keys and constraints.  

3. Insert at least 10 records in each table with meaningful data.  

4. Display all customer names and their corresponding cities.  

5. Retrieve only unique city names from the customers table.  

6. Show products that cost more than 5000.  

7. List all orders made between specific dates (for example: June to August).  

8. Display all customers who are from a specific country (for example: India).  

9. Sort all products by price in descending order.  

10. Count the total number of customers from each country.

11. Calculate the total sales amount from the orders table.  

12. Find the average product price for each category.  

13. Show all products with price greater than the average price of all products. 

14. Retrieve all customers who have placed an order. 

15. Retrieve all customers who have not placed any order.  

16. Display a list of customer names with their corresponding order IDs using an INNER JOIN.  

17. Show all customers and their orders using a LEFT JOIN.  

18. Show all products that have not been ordered using a RIGHT or LEFT JOIN.  

19. Combine all customer and order data, including unmatched rows, using a FULL JOIN (or UNION method).  

20. Retrieve total quantity of each product sold using GROUP BY with a JOIN. 

21. Find which city has generated the highest total sales.  

22. Retrieve customers along with the count of their total orders
.  
23. Display top 3 customers based on total spending using ORDER BY and LIMIT.  

24. Find customers who purchased products in the “Electronics” category.  

25. Retrieve all pairs of customers from the same country (using SELF JOIN).  

26. Identify which product category contributes the highest total revenue. 

27. Display the latest order date for each customer. 

28. List all products ordered more than 5 times. 

29. Find the customer who placed the largest single order. 
 
30. Retrieve all customers whose names start with the letter ‘A’.
