# Data Analyst Tasks in MySQL

## Beginner Level Tasks
1. Create a database named `sales_analysis` and use it.  
2. Create tables `customers` and `orders` with appropriate fields.  
3. Insert at least 10 customers and 20 orders.  
4. Retrieve all orders with price greater than 500.  
5. List all customers from the country “India”.  
6. Display top 5 orders with the highest total price.  
7. Show all orders placed in the year 2024.  
8. Retrieve all orders where quantity is greater than 5 and price is less than 1000.  
9. Find the total sales amount for each customer.  
10. Count how many orders each customer has placed.  

## Intermediate Level Tasks
11. Display customer name, product name, quantity, and price by joining `customers` and `orders`.  
12. Find the total revenue generated by each customer.  
13. Show customers who have not placed any orders.  
14. Find all orders that are above the average order value.  
15. List customers who spent more than the overall average sales.  
16. Find the top 3 customers by total revenue.  
17. Retrieve the most ordered product name.  
18. Get total sales for each city.  
19. Display total number of unique products ordered.  
20. Show the month with the highest total sales.  

## Advanced Level Tasks
21. Find customers who placed more than 3 orders.  
22. Retrieve customers who have ordered multiple distinct products.  
23. Calculate cumulative sales per customer in ascending order of date.  
24. Find the percentage contribution of each customer to total sales.  
25. Identify customers who didn’t order anything in the last 3 months.  
26. Display top 5 cities by average order value.  
27. Create a view showing customer name and their total purchase value.  
28. Create a stored procedure to return total sales between two given dates.  
29. Create a trigger that updates a log table whenever a new order is inserted.  
30. Create a report showing total sales, average sales, and number of customers per country.
